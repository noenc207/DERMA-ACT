# Methodological Limitations & Scientific Scope Boundaries

This companion repository accompanies a **retrospective computational comparative study** on the curated ISIC 2019 dermoscopic benchmark. Nothing in this codebase constitutes a cleared medical device, validated clinical triage tool, or prospective diagnostic system.

---

## 1. Key Study Boundaries Reflected in This Codebase

1. **No Patient-Level Identifiers:** ISIC 2019 Training Metadata provides `lesion_id` for `16,249 / 25,331` images; the remaining `9,082` images lack a `lesion_id` entry and are grouped operationally as single-image units (`13,931` operational partition groups total: `4,849` observed `lesion_id` clusters + `9,082` singleton images; test split `N = 3,686`, `K = 1,972` groups). Shared-patient clustering across distinct lesions is therefore unobserved.
2. **Retrospective Single-Dataset Scope Only:** All evaluated models, policy parameters, and conformal quantiles derive exclusively from retrospective ISIC 2019 partitions. No external clinical cohort (e.g., prospective clinic acquisition, multi-site hardware domain shift) is claimed or redistributed in this release.
3. **Action Inventory Asymmetry & Post-Hoc Masking (`Phase F`):** Learned Policy V1 was trained on the full nine-action space `A1`–`A9`, whereas the validation-ranked fixed sequence uses five spatial crops (`A6 -> A5 -> A3 -> A1 -> A4`). Candidate masking restricts V1 to `{A1, A3, A4, A5, A6}` at test time without policy retraining (`26.21%` error, `3.44` mean views vs. `3.90` for fixed confidence stopping, Holm-Bonferroni `p_adj = 1.000`). This isolates action-space confounding under frozen weights but does not substitute for training a 5-action policy from scratch.
4. **Equal-Inventory Comparator Parity (`CAL-Stop`):** At `B = 5`, full Learned Policy V1 (`26.94%` Top-1 error) does not outperform the validation-ranked fixed sequence with confidence stopping (`25.96%` error, `74.04%` accuracy, `p_adj = 0.319`), instead exhibiting diagnostic-stratum trade-offs (`AKIEC +9.05 pp` sensitivity vs. `NV -3.10 pp` and `BKL -5.78 pp`).
5. **Virtual Operator Numerical Artifacts (`A7`, `A9`):**
   - `A7` (high-frequency Gabor bank) applies `np.uint8` saturation clipping prior to pooling, leading to `99.97%` median pixel saturation and `25.69%` zero-collapse on validation, and is selected in only `6 / 14,815` (`0.04%`) test steps (`0.16%` of episodes).
   - `A9` is a deterministic non-semantic morphological dark-structure closing/inpainting mask (`11.55%` mean masked area in validation) rather than validated anatomical hair removal.
6. **Synthetic Perturbation Scope (`Figure 8`):** Controlled single-view (`A0`) Pillow/geometric perturbations across `16` transforms (`N = 50` validation images) probe algorithmic sensitivity only and do not prove real-world optical or imaging-hardware invariance.
7. **Instability Head Calibration & Operational Caveat:** The pre-acquisition instability head ranks realized sequential Top-1 decision flips (`M = 14,815` transitions, `988` flips = `6.67%`; `AUROC = 0.9473`, `AP = 0.5172`) but **neither prevents sequential prediction flips nor constitutes a validated clinical alert mechanism**.
8. **Conformal Minority Under-Coverage (`Phase G` & `P7.1`):** Global split-conformal LAC (`alpha = 0.10`, `k* = 634`, `q* = 0.904267` on `D_conf N = 703`) yields `90.67%` marginal test coverage (`mean set size = 1.92`), yet exhibits `74.21%` coverage on `AKIEC`. Class-conditional Mondrian conformal calibration fails to resolve this gap (`70.59%` `AKIEC` test coverage, `2.39` overall mean set size) due to severe finite-sample variance in rare calibration strata (`AKIEC` `N_conf = 17`!).
9. **Fixed Unit Cost Proxy:** Evaluation reports mean virtual-view acquisitions (`1.0` unit per acquired view) rather than prospective bedside latency, compute power, or clinician reading cost.
10. **Historical Backbone Training Metadata:** Early backbone training checkpoints omit fine-grained seed recovery for historical pre-freeze optimization runs; downstream evaluation operates deterministically on frozen logits, canonical configs, and seeded (`seed = 20261112`) bootstrap audits.
